Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

To generate your personalized donation certificate, we collect the following personal information explicitly provided by you:

  • Full Name: To personalize the certificate.
  • Phone Number: For identification and contact regarding your donation.
  • Email Address: To send updates or a copy of your certificate.
  • Photograph: To visually personalize the certificate background.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed for the sole purposes of:

  • Generating and allowing you to download your custom "Watch Donation" certificate.
  • Recording your pledge/donation in our campaign database.
  • Contacting you regarding the logistics of your watch donation.

We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your information is stored securely in our database (Google Sheets/Drive) managed by the campaign administrators. Images may be processed temporally in your browser for preview generation.

4. Consent

By using this website and checking the consent box, you explicitly agree to the collection and processing of your data as described in this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or wish to request the deletion of your data, please contact the campaign organizers.
